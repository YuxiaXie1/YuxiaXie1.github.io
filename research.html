LEARN PROGRAMS

Structured Neural Bottlenecks
FOUNDATION MODELS · INTERPRETABILITY

Structure makes
biology readable.

Foundation models capture rich representations of single-cell data, but these embeddings are often opaque. I am developing structured bottleneck architectures that decompose high-dimensional embeddings into interpretable cell and tissue programs while preserving residual variation for discovery.

THE QUESTION

Which programs mark relapse-associated states?

05

VIRTUAL CELLS

Conditional Flow Matching
PERTURBATION BIOLOGY · TRANSFER LEARNING

Same perturbation.
Different contexts.

Cells respond to drugs, niches, and disease signals differently depending on patient context. Using conditional flow matching, I model within-state changes and abundance shifts to separate shared drug responses from patient-specific variation — enabling virtual experiments that predict how individual tumors will respond to treatment.

THE QUESTION

Which responses transfer across patients?

06

VIRTUAL TISSUES

Multimodal Generative Fields
GENERATIVE MODELS · CLINICAL TRANSLATION

From routine samples
to spatial insight.

Routine clinical specimens — H&E images, targeted panels, and DNA sequencing — each capture partial views of tissue biology. I am building generative models that learn measurement operators connecting these modalities, reconstructing spatial programs with calibrated uncertainty from the data most hospitals already collect.

THE QUESTION
