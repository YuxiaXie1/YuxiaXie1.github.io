BIOLOGICAL REPRESENTATION

Make pathway activity
more reliable.

Gene co-expression networks can provide structural clues that conventional pathway scores miss. I develop topology-aware approaches to improve pathway activity scoring in single-cell and spatial data, including xGATE.

MULTIMODAL LEARNING

Bring image and gene data
into conversation.

My work on multimodal integration uses pathology images and targeted gene panels to impute gene expression in spatial transcriptomics. xMINT explores how cross-modal transfer can make richer biological inference possible from limited measurements.

CANCER & TRANSLATIONAL SCIENCE

Understand the pathways
behind tumor diversity.

As PI of an NIH/NCI K99–R00 award, I am developing SPACT and SPACT-ST: interpretable AI frameworks that combine molecular networks with tissue architecture to infer pathway activity in single-cell and spatial transcriptomics.
